Speak a thought

Tap the microphone and talk. Tap again when you're done. Review the text, fix anything it misheard, then save.

Voice capture isn't supported in this browser. It works in Chrome and Edge (desktop and Android). Safari may not support it. On iPhone, you can still tap the text box below and use the 🎤 key on your keyboard to dictate.

Capture a YouTube video

Paste the link of a video you watched. Then grab its transcript from YouTube and paste it here.

How to get the transcript (browsers aren't allowed to fetch it for you yet. That arrives in Level 3):
  1. Click Get Transcript below. The video opens in a new tab.
  2. Under the video, click ...more in the description, then Show transcript.
  3. Click inside the transcript panel, select all the text (drag, or Ctrl+A / Cmd+A while it's focused), and copy it.
  4. Come back here and paste it into the box below.
